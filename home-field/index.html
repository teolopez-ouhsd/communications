Game coverage

At least one game every week

District media is working toward covering at least one varsity game each week across our 13 schools. Coverage is shared fairly across schools and sports over the season, so every varsity program gets its turn.

What we capture

Action photography, team moments, and short video when time allows. Coverage is for district and school communications.

How games are chosen

We cover varsity games only. We look at the weekly varsity schedule, rotate schools and sports, and consider rivalry games, senior nights, and playoff games.

What coaches can do

Send your schedule early and flag special events like senior night, rivalry games, and playoffs. Our team stays neutral across all schools.

Want us at a game? Email teo.lopez@oxnardunion.org with the date, opponent, time, and location of your varsity game as early as you can. We cannot promise every request, but it helps us plan the week.

Student-athlete spotlight

One game, one featured student-athlete

At each game we cover, we feature one varsity student-athlete from the school we visit. Here is how the process works from start to finish.

  1. We pick the gameWe choose the week's game from the schedule and let the athletic director and head coach know we are coming.
  2. Your coaches choose the student-athleteWhoever your coaching staff picks is who we feature. We do not choose for you, and you do not need to send us anything by email.
  3. The athletic director helps us connectWe ask the athletic director to help us reach your coaching staff so we can have the student-athlete before the game starts.
  4. Before the game: a mini photo sessionWe confirm the student-athlete's name spelling, grade, jersey number, and position, take a quick mini photo session, and ask you for a few words.
  5. We publish the highlightThe featured student-athlete is shared on the district's Facebook and Instagram accounts, with the school tagged, and the full gallery goes to the coach.

What we ask you at the game

No emails or forms. We ask in person, before the game starts.

  • The student-athlete's name, so we can confirm the spelling
  • Grade, jersey number, and position
  • What is something special you would like to share about this student-athlete?
  • Why is this student-athlete your pick for this spotlight?

How we choose fairly

  • We rotate across schools and sports
  • We aim to feature different athletes, not repeat the same few
  • Character, effort, and team impact count as much as stats
  • Student photo permissions are checked before anything is posted
Athletic directors: Your help makes this work. Please connect us with the coaching staff ahead of game day so the student-athlete is ready before the game starts.
Photo permissions: We only feature students whose families have not opted out of media release. If you know a student should not be photographed or posted, tell us before game day.

Tag the district

Tag us in every post about your team

Whenever your team or school posts about your sport, please tag the district. It lets us see what you are sharing, reshare and repost your content, and keep track of your schedule so we can plan which games to attend.

Upcoming schedules

Posting a weekly schedule, a game day graphic, or a senior night announcement? Tag us so the game lands on our radar early.

Game day

Pregame hype, lineups, live updates, and photos from the stands all count. Tag us while the game is happening.

After the game

Final scores, recaps, and thank you posts. Tag us whether you won or lost. Every result is worth sharing.

How to tag

  • Tag @oxnardunion on Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube. It is the same handle on all three
  • On Instagram, tag us in the caption or on the photo itself
  • Add your school's team hashtag if you have one
  • Keep your account public when posting game content so the tag reaches us

Why it matters

  • We can reshare your posts to the whole district community
  • We see schedules ahead of time and plan coverage around them
  • We can find your photos and results without chasing them down
  • Every school gets equal visibility, big win or tough loss
Quick habit: Schedule post, game day post, final score post. Tag the district on all three and we will be able to follow your season from start to finish.

Logo use

Use your own school's team logo

Each school has its own brand style guide and approved team logo set. When you make flyers, graphics, or posts for your team, follow your school's guide and use your school's team logos, not the district logo.

Do

  • Find your school's brand style guide and download your team logos from it
  • Follow your school's rules for color, clear space, and backgrounds
  • Use the full color version on light backgrounds and the one-color or white version on dark ones
  • Use vector files (SVG or PDF) for print and PNG for screens

Do not

  • Stretch, squash, rotate, or recolor a logo
  • Add shadows, outlines, or effects
  • Screenshot a logo from social media or a website
  • Use a retired logo or an old version from a previous design
Not sure which logo to use? Check your school's guide first. Retired logos are marked in each guide and should not be used on new materials. If something is missing, reach out and we will help you find it.

AI generated flyers

Better flyers with better prompts

AI tools can speed up game day flyers, but they work best when you give them clear direction and then add the real logos yourself.

Tips for stronger results

  • Name the sport, the event, the date, time, and place in the prompt
  • Ask for the school colors by name and hex value
  • Choose a style: bold, clean, high energy, or classic
  • Ask for simple layouts with room for a logo
  • Generate a background, then add text and logos in Canva or another editor

Always check before sharing

  • Spelling of every name, date, and time
  • That the logo is the official file, not an AI imitation
  • That no real student's face or likeness was invented
  • That text is readable and has strong contrast
Important: AI tools often redraw or distort logos and mascots. Never let AI create the logo. Leave a blank space and place the official logo file on top afterward.
Starter prompt
Create a bold, high energy game day flyer background for high school varsity football. Use [school color 1] and [school color 2] with a dark textured background and diagonal light streaks. Leave a clear empty area at the top for a logo and a text area at the bottom for the date, time, and opponent. No text, no logos, no people.

Photo galleries

Using our photos the right way

Photo galleries are shared with coaches for team and school use. These guidelines help keep our students and our work protected.

UseGuidance
Team and school social mediaWelcome. Tag the district and credit the photographer.
Athletic program, banquets, newslettersWelcome for school and team purposes.
Sharing with student-athletes and familiesWelcome. Please share the gallery link or download rather than reposting edited copies.
Editing or filteringLight cropping is fine. Please avoid heavy filters, added effects, or AI alteration.
Selling photos or merchandiseNot permitted without written approval.
Media outlets and outside publicationsContact district media first so we can confirm credit and permissions.
For the full policy, read the Photo and Video Terms of Use.

Crediting photos

Use a simple credit line wherever the photo appears:

Photo: OUHSD

If a photo was taken by a named photographer, use the credit given in the gallery.

Student privacy

Do not tag minors by full name outside approved school channels, and take a photo down promptly if a family asks. Photos should never be paired with personal details like addresses or phone numbers.

Common questions

Can I post photos before the district does?
Yes, once the gallery is shared with you. We ask that you credit the photographer and avoid altering the images.
How long until a gallery is ready?
Galleries are typically shared within a few days of the game. Busy weeks may take a little longer.
What if I need a specific photo?
Send the game, jersey number, and moment you are looking for and we will check the gallery.
