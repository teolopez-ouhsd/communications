Video walkthrough, about 24 seconds. Step 1: pick your school from the list. Step 2: copy the ready-made ParentSquare message, with the survey links included, and paste it into a new post. Step 3: download the English and Spanish flyers, each with a QR code, and upload them to social media.

1
Step 1

Pick your school

Your school's name is added to the message and to both flyers.

Skip this to keep the district-wide wording.

2
Step 2

Post to ParentSquare

Copy the subject line and message, then paste them into a new ParentSquare post. The survey links are hyperlinks, so no long web addresses show.

Subject line
Message
Tip: after pasting, check that the survey lines show as links. If ParentSquare drops them, highlight each line and use its link button to add the web address. You can also attach a flyer from Step 3 to the same post.
3
Step 3

Share on social media

Download the English and Spanish posts and upload them to Instagram, Facebook or your school account. Each one has a QR code that opens the survey. Post both for the widest reach.
