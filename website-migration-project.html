Overview

One project, one place to follow it

OUHSD is redesigning and migrating oxnardunion.org, along with every school site, from our current platform, FinalSite, to Wix. It's the biggest change to the district's web presence in years — new navigation, a design pulled from the same brand system used across this hub, and full compliance with new federal web accessibility requirements.

This page is the one-stop shop for the project: why we picked Wix, what we're trying to accomplish, what accessibility compliance requires of us, the full build-out timeline, and a direct link to the surveys shaping the redesign. Bookmark it — it'll stay current as the project moves forward.

The platform

Why we're moving from FinalSite to Wix

After reviewing our current platform against where the site needs to be, the district chose Wix as the new home for oxnardunion.org and every school site.

Accessibility built inWix's editor and templates are built around modern accessibility standards, giving us a stronger starting point for meeting WCAG 2.1 AA.
Easier for our own staff to maintainA modern, visual editor means site owners across departments and schools can update content directly, without waiting on a developer for every change.
One consistent design systemPages built in Wix draw from the same colors, type, and components used across this hub, so every page reads as clearly OUHSD.
Built for mobileMost visitors reach our sites from a phone. Wix's responsive tools make that a first-class experience instead of an afterthought.

Compliance

Accessibility & DOJ compliance

The U.S. Department of Justice finalized a rule in 2024 requiring state and local government entities — including public school districts — to make their websites and mobile apps meet the WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standard. A school district isn't automatically placed in the "special district government" category; instead, an independent school district's deadline is based on the population of the area it serves.

Districts serving an area of 50,000 or more people must comply by April 26, 2027 (extended by one year from the original 2026 date under DOJ's April 2026 interim final rule); districts serving fewer than 50,000 have until April 26, 2028. OUHSD serves Oxnard, Camarillo, and Port Hueneme — well above that threshold — which puts our deadline at April 26, 2027.

Meeting this standard touches most of the site — from how images and video are made available to screen readers, to color contrast, keyboard access, and how forms and documents are built. The technical work is being scoped directly against the DOJ's requirements between the Web & Multimedia Coordinator and district counsel, rather than tracked item-by-item on this page.

This is general information, not legal advice. Confirm exact requirements and any applicable exceptions with district legal counsel or your accessibility compliance office. Read the DOJ's official fact sheet on this rule →
