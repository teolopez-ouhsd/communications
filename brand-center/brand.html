Brand overview

One district

Oxnard Union High School District serves high school and adult students across Oxnard, Port Hueneme, Camarillo, and surrounding communities.

Many schools

Each school keeps its own name, mascot, and colors. The district brand connects them.

One standard

Whether it's a flyer or a stadium banner, families should recognize it as ours.

District brand or school brand?

If it's about...Use
Enrollment, the board, district programs, or more than one schoolDistrict brand
One school's events, athletics, clubs, or spirit wearThat school's brand
A school document that also needs the district (like letterhead)School brand first, district logo small in the footer

Voice and tone

Draft

Our voice stays the same. Our tone shifts with the moment.

MomentToneExample
CelebrationsWarm, proud"Congratulations to our Class of 2027 graduates!"
Everyday updatesFriendly, direct"Registration opens Monday. Here's what you need."
Urgent or safetyCalm, factual, brief"All students are safe. Here is what happened and what to expect."

Naming and terminology

Required

Do

  • Oxnard Union High School District on first mention
  • OUHSD or "the District" after that
  • Full school names on first mention, like Pacifica High School

Don't

  • Oxnard Union School District, OUSD, or Oxnard Unified
  • O.U.H.S.D. with periods
  • Nicknames for schools in formal pieces
