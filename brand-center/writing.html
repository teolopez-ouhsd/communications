Abbreviations and acronyms

Recommended
  • Spell it out the first time, with the acronym after: Advanced Placement (AP).
  • No periods in acronyms: OUHSD, AP, CTE.
  • Avoid acronyms families may not know, like LCAP or IEP, without explaining them.

Titles

Recommended
  • Capitalize a title before a name: Principal Jane Smith.
  • Lowercase after a name: Jane Smith, principal of Pacifica High School.
  • Use "Dr." only for earned doctorates, on first mention.

Voice and tone

Recommended
  • Short sentences. Plain words. Aim for an 8th grade reading level for family messages.
  • Lead with what families need to do, then the details.
  • Use "you" and "we."
  • Dates: Monday, Oct. 12, 2026. Times: 6 p.m., 6:30 p.m.

English and Spanish communication

Required
  • Messages for all families go out in English and Spanish.
  • Use professional or district-approved translation. Machine translation must be reviewed by a fluent speaker before sending.
  • Give both languages equal weight: same size, same placement, same details.
  • On flyers, place languages side by side or on front and back. Don't shrink the Spanish.
  • Keep names of schools and programs in their official form in both languages.
