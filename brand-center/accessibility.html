Accessibility

Everything we publish should work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, captions, or magnification. It's also the law.

Color contrast

Required

4.5:1

Normal text, like paragraphs and captions.

3:1

Large text (24 px, or 19 px bold), icons, buttons, and form borders.

Never color alone

Add a label, icon, or underline so meaning isn't lost.

Accessible typography

Required
  • Body text: at least 16 px on screens, 11 pt in print, 20 pt on slides.
  • Left-align paragraphs. Don't justify or center long text.
  • Avoid all caps for more than a few words.
  • Don't put text over busy photos.

Alt text

Required

Good

"Pacifica seniors in green caps and gowns toss their caps on the football field."

Not helpful

"IMG_4021.jpg" or "Photo of graduation"

  • Describe what matters in one or two sentences.
  • Don't start with "image of."
  • If the image has text, like a flyer, include that text.
  • Logos: "Oxnard Union High School District logo." Decorative shapes: leave alt text empty.

Video captions

Required

Captions on every video, checked for accuracy. Livestreams get live captions when possible, and corrected captions on the recording. Caption details

Links

Required

Do

Don't

To see the bell schedule, click here.

Links should be underlined, and say when they open a PDF or a new tab.

Documents

Required
  • Use the built-in Heading 1, Heading 2 styles, in order.
  • Use real bulleted and numbered lists.
  • Add alt text to images. Use simple tables with a header row.
  • Run the accessibility checker in Word (Review tab) or Google Docs (Grackle add-on) before sharing.

PDFs

Required
  • Post a web page instead of a PDF whenever you can.
  • Never post a scanned image of text. Export from the original document.
  • Export with tags: in Word, use Save as PDF with "Document structure tags for accessibility" checked.
  • Set the document title and language.

Social media

Required

Alt text on every image, captions on every video, CamelCase hashtags, and key details in the post text. Social checklist

Website content

Required
  • Headings in order, descriptive links, alt text, captioned video.
  • Tables only for data, never for layout.
  • Forms have visible labels.
  • Check pages with a free tool like WAVE before publishing.

WCAG and ADA

Required

Public school districts must make websites, documents, and media accessible under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Section 504. The technical standard is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. The U.S. Department of Justice's 2024 rule requires this standard for state and local government web content, with compliance dates starting in 2026 for larger entities. Confirm the date that applies to OUHSD with district leadership.
