Which file should I use?

Recommended
SVGPrint shops, sign and apparel vendors, and anything scaled up. It stays sharp at any size. If a vendor asks for EPS, AI, or PDF, send the SVG.
PNGSlides, Canva, Word, Google Docs, websites, and social posts. Transparent background, so it sits cleanly on any color.
JPGOnly when a system won't accept PNG, like some email or form uploads. It has a solid background (white, or Oxnard Blue for the white logo).

Clear space

Required
xxxx

x = the height of the capital letters in OXNARD UNION.

Keep at least x of empty space on every side. Text, photos, other logos, and the edge of the page stay out of that zone. When the logo gets bigger or smaller, the clear space scales with it.

Incorrect logo use

Required

The logo's shape, colors, and parts are fixed. If you're dragging a corner, changing a color, or adding an effect, stop.

Co-branding with partners

RecommendedDraft

Side-by-side logos

Partner logo
  • Place logos side by side, separated by a thin line.
  • Make them look the same size, not one dominating.
  • Keep each logo's clear space.

Before you co-brand

  • Get approval from Communications before sharing the district logo with any outside group.
  • Sponsors: use "Supported by" or "In partnership with" plus their logo, smaller than ours.
  • Never merge our logo with another logo into a new mark.
