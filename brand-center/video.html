Video

Short, captioned, and clearly ours. These standards apply to social clips, YouTube, and livestreams.

Video branding

Recommended

Keep it short

Social: 15 to 60 seconds. Feature stories: 1 to 3 minutes.

Hook first

Open on the best moment. Branding can wait for the end.

Brand fonts and colors

Montserrat titles, Open Sans text, Oxnard Blue or school colors.

Logo placement

Required
Small, in a cornerWhite logo, bottom or top right, about 15 to 20% of the frame width.
  • Use the white logo over footage and the full-color logo on white end cards.
  • Keep it out of faces and away from captions.
  • Use the school logo for school stories, the district logo for district stories.
  • Don't animate, spin, or recolor the logo.

Intros and outros

Recommended
  • Intro: 3 seconds or less, or skip it on social.
  • Outro (end card): logo, website, and social handles for 3 to 5 seconds.
  • Use the approved templates once they're available.

Lower thirds

Recommended
Maria Gonzalez
Senior, Pacifica High School
Name on line one, title on line twoShow for 4 to 6 seconds the first time someone speaks. Keep inside the safe area.

Titles

Recommended
  • Montserrat ExtraBold, short, sentence case or all caps.
  • Keep text inside the center 90% of the frame so nothing gets cut off.
  • On-screen text stays up long enough to read twice.

Captions

Required
  • Every video gets accurate captions before it's posted. Auto-captions must be reviewed and corrected.
  • Include speaker changes and important sounds, like [crowd cheering].
  • Spanish-language videos get Spanish captions. Add English or Spanish translations for key district messages when possible.
  • Burned-in captions on social: Open Sans SemiBold, white text on a dark box, lower third of the frame.

Interview framing

Recommended
Off-center, looking acrossSubject on one third, looking toward the interviewer just off camera.
  • Eyes on the top third line, chest-up framing.
  • Quiet room, lav mic when possible, light from the front or side.
  • Uncluttered background with a hint of the school.
  • Have the subject repeat the question in their answer.

Livestream graphics

Recommended
  • Holding slide before start: event name, date, and "We'll begin shortly."
  • Small logo bug in a corner throughout. Lower thirds for speakers.
  • End slide with thanks and where to find the recording.
  • Board meetings and graduations: caption the live stream when possible and post the corrected recording within a few days.

Thumbnails

Recommended
  • 1280 × 720 px (16:9).
  • A clear face or action shot, plus up to five words of Montserrat ExtraBold.
  • Small logo in a corner. High contrast; it has to read at phone size.
