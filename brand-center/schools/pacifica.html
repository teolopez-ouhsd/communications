Mascot: the Tritons

Required

The Triton figure is Pacifica's mascot mark. It works on light, green, and dark backgrounds. Always write Tritons, never Titans.

Athletics marks

Required

For teams, uniforms, and game day graphics. Numerals are for jersey and warm-up numbers only. The helmet is for football only, full color only.

Official colors

Required

Pacifica Green leads. Gold is an accent, used sparingly. Tap any value to copy it.

No gold text on green. It measures 2.38:1 and fails even for large headlines. Use white on green (6.93:1) or gold on black (7.20:1).

School-specific guidance

Required

Do

  • Start with the Primary Logo unless your space calls for something else
  • Lettered logos on light backgrounds; Triton or Trident on green or dark
  • Keep clear space around every logo
  • Send vendors the SVG plus PMS 342 C and PMS 131 C

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects
  • Retype PACIFICA TRITONS in a font
  • Put gold text on green
  • Use Numerals or the Helmet outside uniforms and football
