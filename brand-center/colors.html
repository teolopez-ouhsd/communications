Primary palette

Required

Use these exact values. Don't use "navy" or "school red" from a color picker; they drift.

Secondary palette

RecommendedDraft

For backgrounds, charts, and supporting shapes, use tints and shades of Oxnard Blue and Red (below) plus white and near-black text. This keeps everything in the family.

Accent colors

Available
Placeholder: the district hasn't defined accent colors beyond Oxnard Red. Until then, don't add new colors to district materials. Schools use their own official colors.

Accessible color combinations

Required

Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. Large text (24 px, or 19 px bold) and icons need 3:1.

Never put red text on blue, or blue text on red. It measures about 2.2:1 and is hard for everyone to read. Separate them with white.

Tints and shades

Available

Darker shades work for buttons and headers with white text. Light tints work for background fills with dark text. Tap to copy.
