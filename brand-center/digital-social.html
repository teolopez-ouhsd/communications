Website standards

Required
  • Use the site's built-in header, colors, and fonts. Don't add custom styles, colors, or fonts.
  • Use real headings (Heading 2, Heading 3) in order. Don't make text bold to fake a heading.
  • Post information as web page text, not as an image or PDF, whenever you can.
  • Every page has an owner and gets reviewed at least once a year. Remove outdated content.
  • Images get alt text. Videos get captions.

Social profile graphics

Required
  • Use the official logo or school mark, centered with space for the circle crop.
  • Schools use their own simplest mark, like a shield or icon.
  • Same profile picture on every platform.
  • Covers and banners: a real photo, no text-heavy designs.

Social post standards

Recommended

Do

  • Lead with students and photos
  • Keep text on images to a short headline
  • Put details in the caption
  • Post in English and Spanish when the message is for all families
  • Tag the school or district account

Don't

  • Post a flyer packed with small text as the only version
  • Use emojis to replace words
  • Post photos of students who opted out
  • Share personal opinions on official accounts

Hashtags

Recommended
  • Use one to three hashtags. More looks like spam.
  • Write them in CamelCase so screen readers can say them: #ClassOf2027, not #classof2027.
  • Put hashtags at the end of the caption.

Alt text

Required

Add alt text to every image you post. Most platforms have an "Add alt text" option in advanced or accessibility settings. How to write alt text

Captioning

Required

All videos need accurate captions. Review auto-captions before posting, especially names and Spanish words. Caption rules
