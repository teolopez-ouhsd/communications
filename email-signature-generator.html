Why use this instead of a graphic image as your signature?

It's responsiveResizes cleanly on any screen — a signature image just gets awkwardly scaled or clipped.
Info is copyableYour name, phone, and email are real text, so people can select and copy them — not possible from a flat image.
Tap to call or emailOn a phone, your phone number and email are live links — one tap starts a call or a new message.
Accessible by designReal text and labeled images mean screen readers can read your signature, not just look at it — a picture-based signature can't do that.

Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, go to Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → the General tab → Signature.
  3. Click into your signature box and paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  4. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
  5. Send yourself a test email to confirm the photo and links show up correctly.
If the photo doesn't show up after pasting: make sure you clicked Save Changes in Gmail, then send yourself a test email. If it's still missing, contact teo.lopez@oxnardunion.org.

Adjust your photo

Drag to reposition, use the slider to zoom. What's inside the circle is what gets used.
