Athletics

Oxnard Union Athletics — Photo & Video Terms of Use

Provided by the Web & Multimedia Coordinator • Fall 2026

Each week, our Web & Multimedia Coordinator covers a different game across our athletic programs, producing high-resolution photos and video. These files are provided to Athletic Directors and coaches at no cost, for the benefit of our student-athletes and programs. This note covers how they can be used.

Ownership

These images are created as official district work and remain the property of Oxnard Union. Providing them to your program is a permission to use them as described below, not a transfer of ownership — please don't redistribute them outside your program's own use without checking in first.

Approved For

  • Downloading and keeping originals for your program's own records and archives.
  • Sharing with student-athletes and families for personal use — printing, photo books, keepsakes.
  • Posting on your team's or school's official social media, websites, and district channels, with credit.
  • Non-commercial program materials — senior night slideshows, banquet recaps, bulletin boards, yearbook submissions, recruiting profiles for your own athletes.
  • Sharing this same permission with your coaching staff for their team's use.

Not Approved Without Checking First

  • Selling, licensing, or using images in anything for-profit — merchandise, paid ads, sponsorship materials, etc.
  • Passing them to outside organizations, media outlets, third-party recruiting services, or stock/photo-sale sites.
  • Posting them in a way that removes credit or implies a different photographer or source.
  • Heavily editing or using images in a way that could misrepresent a student-athlete or the program.

Credit

When reposting publicly, please credit: "Photo/Video: Oxnard Union Web & Multimedia — Teo Lopez", or tag @oxnardunion if posting on Instagram or similar.

Questions, or a use not covered here? Reach out before assuming — most reasonable program uses are fine, we just want to know how images are being used district-wide: Teo Lopez, Web & Multimedia Coordinator.
