About this project

Why we're asking

The district's current website has grown over many years, with new pages and sections added as needs came up. That's made it harder to find things — for parents looking for a school's contact info, for students trying to register for a class, and for staff trying to point people to the right resource.

Before we redesign the site, we want to hear directly from the people who use it most. Your answers will directly shape the new site's navigation, content, and design — not just background research that goes unused.

Takes about 5–10 minutesA short survey, not a long one — built to respect your time.
One survey per audienceQuestions are tailored to what matters for how you use the site.
