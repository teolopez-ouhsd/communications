Web & Multimedia Coordinator — Website Redesign

Help shape the new OUHSD website

We're redesigning oxnardunion.org, and we want it to work for the people who actually use it. Tell us what's hard to find, what's missing, and what a better version looks like to you.

About this project

Why we're asking

The district's current website has grown over many years, with new pages and sections added as needs came up. That's made it harder to find things — for parents looking for a school's contact info, for students trying to register for a class, and for staff trying to point people to the right resource.

Your answers will directly shape the new site's navigation, content, and design — not just background research that goes unused.

Takes about 5–10 minutesA short survey, not a long one — built to respect your time.
One survey per audienceQuestions are tailored to what matters for how you use the site.
Your input shapes real decisionsResponses directly inform navigation, content, and layout choices.
